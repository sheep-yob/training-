About the Movie

Spider-Man: Across the Spider-Verse follows Miles Morales as he travels through the multiverse and meets a team of Spider-People tasked with protecting its existence. Along the way, he faces difficult choices about fate, family, and what it truly means to be a hero.

  • Jessica Drew

Movie Timeline

  1. Gwen Stacy battles the Vulture in her universe.

  3. Miles is pulled into the Spider Society's headquarters.
  4. Miles clashes with Miguel O'Hara over canon events.
  5. Miles is sent to the wrong universe, setting up the next chapter.

Interesting Facts

This film features over 240 characters from different Spider-Verse universes, making it one of the most ambitious animated films ever made.

Each universe has its own unique art style, giving the movie a visually distinct look for every world Miles visits.

Fun detail: The film took nearly four years to produce due to its complex animation techniques.

It was praised as one of the best-reviewed animated sequels in recent years.
