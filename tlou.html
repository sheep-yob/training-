What Is The Last of Us?

The Last of Us is an action-adventure game set in a United States ravaged by a fungal outbreak that turns the infected into hostile, mindless creatures. It follows Joel, a hardened survivor, and Ellie, a teenage girl who may hold the key to a cure, as they journey across the country facing both the infected and other desperate survivors.

Beyond the survival and combat, the game is best known for its story — a tense, emotional look at loss, trust, and what people are willing to do to protect the ones they love.
